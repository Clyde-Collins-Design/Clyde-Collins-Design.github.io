Ideation


Paper Prototypes

Using the cardboard form as a reference, I stacked 1" slices of pink foam and began carving. The result was ARG-1: majestic, purple, and destined to be slathered in joint compound. Joint compound was a new material to me, but it could be layered over a carved form to give it a tough exterior. The process was quite effective, but the question still remained: how do I make this into full-scale furniture?


Drafting

The most viable option for fabrication was constructing the seating portion from poplar and MDF, then stacking and joining the pink foam to form the non-structural elements.
